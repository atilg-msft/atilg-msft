What We Do

Two ways we work

  • Go-to-market and pricing strategy

B2C

SaaS Services for Hobbyists

Simple, affordable software built for people making things for the love of it, not a paycheck.

  • Lightweight tools for personal projects
  • No-friction pricing for individual users
  • Built for creativity, not enterprises

Why not just ask an AI? A model can summarize what's already public. It can't take a position, weigh trade-offs against your specific constraints, or tell you which finding actually matters for the decision in front of you. That's the part you're paying a human for — research turned into judgment, not just a longer report.

Approach

How an engagement works

A straightforward process, built around getting to a decision — not producing a deck.

01

30-minute intro call

We hear the idea, the market, and where you're stuck. No cost, no obligation — just enough to know if we're useful to you.

02

Market & competitive analysis

We size the market, map who else is solving this problem, and identify where the real openings are.

03

Positioning & strategy

We turn the research into a specific position: who it's for, why you win, and what to say no to.

04

Actionable roadmap

You leave with a concrete plan and clear next steps, not a report that sits in a folder.
