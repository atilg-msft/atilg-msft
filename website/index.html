Who we are

Gen Idea Ltd. was founded to give DIYers and hobbyists the same quality of tools that big companies take for granted — without the enterprise price tag, the bloat, or the sales pitch. We build for people making things for the love of it, not a paycheck. Everything starts from the same question: what makes this a genuinely good experience for the person using it?
